Check out Dhavide Aruliah, Quansight’s Director of Education, on stage at JupyterCon 2023 discussing his experience teaching and training with Jupyter in academia and business.

[JupyterCon](https://www.jupytercon.com/) is an annual industry event for data scientists, business analysts, researchers, educators, developers, and core project maintainers working within the Jupyter ecosystem. Quansight’s Dhavide Aruliah recently [presented at JupyterCon 2023](https://cfp.jupytercon.com/2023/talk/ZQBNTV/) in Paris, France.

Drawing from a decade of experience in academia, software bootcamps, and training engagements for corporate and government clients, Dhavide shared first-hand insights on teaching with Jupyter, the tools and techniques he used, and the lessons learned from 10+ years in the Python space. Among other things, he discussed:

- The importance of tailoring training experiences to the way our brains naturally work, by approaches such as minimizing cognitive load, crafting low-stakes, in-context assessments, and integrating spaced repetition.
- The steady advances in user interface, package/environment management, and platform hosting tools that have greatly simplified and improved the training experience for both instructors and students.
- The similarities and differences between teaching in an academic setting versus training in corporate environments.
